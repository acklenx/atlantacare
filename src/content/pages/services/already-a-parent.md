    ## Empowering Women to Know Their Rights

    Since 1982, Atlanta Care Center has empowered pregnant women to make informed decisions about their pregnancies. Our experienced medical team and staff will walk you through all your options and answer any questions you have about parenting, adoption, and abortion. You shouldn’t feel pressure from anyone to make decisions about your pregnancy that you’re uncomfortable with. We are here to help you.

If you’re already a parent, news of an unintended pregnancy can be difficult to accept. The team at [Atlanta Care Center](/about/) knows the unique challenges of parenting. If an unintended pregnancy is going to complicate your life and cause additional financial or relational stress, you don’t have to go through this uncertain time alone. [Our compassionate staff is here to support you](/team/) through this journey and empower you to make informed choices about your pregnancy.
