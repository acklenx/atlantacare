    ## Essential Services

    If you choose to keep your pregnancy, Atlanta Care Center offers essential services to help you through the pregnancy and early years of motherhood. We know it’s challenging to support a new baby, and we’ve designed our services to support your needs.

    Some of our many support services include:

    **OB/GYN Referrals**

    Our medical team connects you with an OB/GYN that accepts your medical insurance or Medicaid to ensure you are getting the prenatal care you need.

    **Clothing Closet**

    Atlanta Care Center has a clothing closet available for qualified expecting mothers. We offer a range of maternity clothing, baby, and infant clothing up to 2T.

    **Safe Housing Referrals**

    If you’re in an unsafe or violent relationship, we can connect you with community resources to help you find safe housing. You shouldn’t have to live in fear, and Atlanta Care Center is committed to supporting women through even the toughest situations.
