    ## Common Early Pregnancy Symptoms

    The most common early symptom of pregnancy is a missed period. Some other early signs include:

    - Backaches
    - Darkening of nipples
    - Fatigue
    - Food cravings or aversions
    - Frequent urination
    - Headaches
    - Nausea
    - Slight bleeding
    - Swollen or tender breasts

An unexpected pregnancy can be scary, but you aren’t alone! The compassionate staff at Atlanta Care Center is here to support you. However, before you start planning your next steps, you need to make sure you’re actually pregnant.

There are numerous early signs of pregnancy. It is possible that some of these symptoms can be caused by medical conditions other than pregnancy. The best way to verify that you are pregnant is by taking a laboratory-grade pregnancy test. Atlanta Care Center offers [free of charge pregnancy tests and consultations](/services/free-pregnancy-test/) to women in Atlanta.
