    ## Free of Charge Pregnancy Testing, Consultations, and Ultrasounds

    Since 1982, [Atlanta Care Center](/about/) has offered [free of charge pregnancy testing and consultation](/services/free-pregnancy-test/) to women facing unexpected pregnancies. While you may have taken an at-home pregnancy test, you may need to take one from a medical professional before making a decision about abortion. We also provide proof of pregnancy.

    An abortion provider will need to know your personal medical history and the age of the fetus before performing an abortion. Ultrasounds are a reliable method of confirming a pregnancy and determining gestational age. Atlanta Care Center offers free of charge ultrasounds to qualified patients.

    ## Empowering Women to Make Informed Choices

    Since 1982, Atlanta Care Center has empowered women to make informed decisions about their pregnancy and abortion. Our experienced, compassionate medical team and staff will walk you through all your abortion options and can answer your questions. You should never feel pressured to make decisions about your pregnancy that you’re uncomfortable with. We are here to support you during this challenging time.

    During your appointment, you can expect compassionate, non-judgmental, factual, and evidence-based information about your abortion options.

When facing an unintended pregnancy, abortion is an option. Choosing to have an abortion is a highly personal choice, and only you can make the final decision. Before you begin processing your abortion options, you need to confirm that you are actually pregnant with a medical professional.
