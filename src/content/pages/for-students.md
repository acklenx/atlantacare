An unintended pregnancy can be overwhelming for students, but you don’t have to walk through this time alone. Since 1982, the compassionate professionals at Atlanta Care Center have served students facing an unexpected pregnancy. We offer safe and free of charge pregnancy services to women in the Atlanta area, even women under 18 years old.

Our medical team regularly serves students that attend:

- Atlanta Area High Schools
- Cobb Area High Schools
- Clark-Atlanta College
- Emory University
- Georgia State University
- Georgia Tech
- Kennesaw State University
- Savannah College of Art and Design
- Spelman College
