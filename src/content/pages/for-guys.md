    ## Guidance for Guys

    Everyone reacts differently in complicated situations, and you should give yourself time to process the emotions of an unexpected pregnancy.

    Here’s some guidance on what you should be sure to do:

    **Stay Calm**

    It’s easy to feel overwhelmed in these situations, but it’s critical to keep your emotions in check and allow yourself to process the situation.

    **Know the Facts**

    Before you begin worrying over an unplanned pregnancy, you should make sure she’s actually pregnant. We offer [free of charge pregnancy testing and consultations](/services/free-pregnancy-test/) to women in Atlanta.

    **Avoid Pressuring**

    Remember that you aren’t the only one involved in this situation. If you pressure her to make decisions that she’s uncomfortable with, you may create more tension.
